# Prompts de fotos de producto · La Pizza de Nonno

Un prompt completo por producto. Se adjunta siempre la foto de las **baldosas blancas**; si además se adjunta la foto real de esa pizza, el prompt le pide que la reproduzca tal cual.

Para Instagram, cambia `Square 1:1` por `Vertical 4:5`.

## Pizzas clásicas

### Margarita

```
Professional food photograph of the "Margarita" pizza from La Pizza de Nonno, a local pizzeria in Murcia, freshly baked.

Reference images: the attached photo of white tiles is the background. If another photo of this pizza is also attached, reproduce exactly that pizza: the same toppings, the same amount and placement, the same crust shape and the same cheese, only removing any cardboard box, tray or paper. If no photo of the pizza is attached, follow the description below.

The pizza: Neapolitan-style hand-stretched dough, about 33 cm, with a thick, puffy, golden crust full of air bubbles and small charred leopard spots, tomato sauce, mozzarella and a few fresh basil leaves. Nothing else on top.

Make it look handmade and irregular, never perfect: a slightly uneven, not perfectly round shape, crust thicker in some places than others, some air bubbles bigger than others, cheese melted and browned unevenly, toppings scattered naturally and not symmetrically, a few pieces close to the edge.

Setting: the pizza sits directly on a light-grey stone countertop. Behind it, the white glossy tiled wall from the attached background image, with thin light-grey grout, softly out of focus.

Camera: low three-quarter angle, about 35 degrees above the table, 70 mm lens. The pizza is centred and fills about 75 % of the frame. The front crust is sharp in the foreground and the centre of the pizza is fully visible, with a gentle depth of field towards the back edge.

Light: soft natural window light from the left, warm, subtle highlights on the melted toppings, a soft contact shadow under the pizza, a faint wisp of steam.

Style: realistic commercial pizzeria photography, true-to-life colours, appetising, crisp detail on the crust texture. Square 1:1.

Avoid: text, logos, watermarks, hands, cutlery, cardboard box, plates, other food, any ingredient not listed, basil or rocket unless listed, cartoon or 3D-render look, plastic-looking surfaces, oversaturated colours, perfectly round shape, perfectly symmetrical toppings, thin cracker crust.
```
